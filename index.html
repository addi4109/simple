Birds

Parrot

The parrot is a colorful bird known for its curved beak and ability to mimic human speech. It is usually green, but can also have red, blue, or yellow feathers. Parrots are intelligent and social birds that live in forests and often eat fruits, seeds, and nuts.

Peacock

The peacock is a large, colorful bird known for its beautiful feather display. The male has long, bright tail feathers with eye-like patterns that it spreads into a fan during courtship. It is mainly found in forests and near villages in India and is known for its loud calls and graceful walk.

KingFisher

The kingfisher is a small, colorful bird found near rivers and lakes. It has a long, sharp beak and catches fish by diving into water. It is known for its quick movement and excellent eyesight, often perching quietly above water kingfisher.
